Over at Commoncog, Cedric Chin has a wonderful piece about [what to pay attention to when it comes to AI](https://commoncog.com/how-to-make-sense-of-ai/). His thinking centers around the idea of sensemaking, which he defines as _the art of knowing what to ignore_—a phrase that melted a knot in my stomach I hardly realized was there. 

I’m not someone who needs to stay up-to-date on every new method and technique (many of which become obsolete in a matter of weeks), but I do find it challenging to consistently ground my assessments of what is happening and how that is impacting leadership and organizational life given the absolute deluge of articles, videos, hot-takes, and deep-dives flooding the information environment. Which makes the first step of Cedric’s methodology so useful: “ignore all opinions, analysis, predictions, fanciful essays ‘from the future’, ‘situational awareness updates’ and scenario forecasts about AI.” Done and done.
